3Pre-flight (before you hit record)

  • Open TrueHire.exe and maximize the window — clean, full-screen capture.
  • Go to Interview Kits → Senior Software Engineer Interview Kit. In the Category weights card, confirm every box reads 1 (especially Accountability). If one is changed from an earlier take, set it back to 1 and click away to save.
  • On the Candidates tab, set Sort to Match Score (it's the default) and select the Senior Software Engineer job filter.
  • Confirm you see three rings: James 67, Maria 63, David 60. If so, you're ready.
Heads up — weights save instantly. The weight box commits the moment you click or tab away (it writes to the kit and re-scores everyone). That's great for the demo, but it means after each take you must reset Accountability back to 1 to re-run the reveal.

5The math behind it (for the curious / a follow-up clip)

How a rating becomes a score

Each skill is rated on a 0–5 evidence scale, where 5 = strongest evidence present. A rating of 0 (insufficient) or unrated is dropped as missing data.

RatingMeaningScore
5Very strong evidence present100
4Strong evidence present80
3Some evidence present60
2Strong evidence not present40
1Very strong evidence not present20
How weights combine them

The match score is a weighted average of the category scores. Unrated categories simply drop out and the remaining weights renormalize — so a missing rating never silently counts as a zero.

score = Σ(weight × categoryScore) ÷ Σ(weight)

Worked example — David with Accountability at weight 5 (other six at weight 1): his 100 on Tolerance now carries 5× the pull of any other skill, lifting his blend from 60 to 76.
